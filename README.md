## One more thing to check before subscribing

IPTV is a way of delivering television over the internet; the legal question depends on whether a provider has the appropriate rights to the content it distributes. The U.S. Copyright Office has noted that unauthorized internet retransmission of copyrighted broadcasts or streams can infringe copyright rights.

That is worth remembering with any IPTV streaming service, especially when a provider advertises a very large mix of live channels, sports, PPV and on-demand content.
